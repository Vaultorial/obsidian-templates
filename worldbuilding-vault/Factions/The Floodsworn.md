# The Floodsworn

The oath that came out of a refusal: no more drownings. Born in a [[Lowmark]] net-shed the night the Ebb Court turned down [[The Lowmark Petition]], it has grown from a grievance into the bay's second power.

## What it wants

- Stated: the Drowning of the Lower Ward ended and tide-law amended openly, with Lowmark voices on the bench that rules it.
- Real: the same — but the movement now contains two honest readings of "ended". The petition wing means *repealed*; the dike-breaker wing means *made impossible*, with crowbars if the vote fails.

## How it works

- Oath and word of mouth; no rolls, no badges — membership is whether your neighbors have heard you swear.
- Pamphlets printed beyond tide-law in [[Hethwick]] and carried down in fish-barrels; runners crossing [[The Causeway of Bells]] at the peal's edge.
- Quartermastery: [[Perrin Lowsmith]]'s barrels, boats and billet-lists, which make the Floodsworn the only body actually prepared for the flood everyone argues about.

## Key members

- [[Bren Callow]] — orator and leader, holding the seam between the wings.
- [[Perrin Lowsmith]] — quartermaster; the hard wing trusts him more than it trusts Bren.
- Unnamed by design: the dike-breakers keep no named leaders, which Bren finds more frightening than if they did.

## Fault lines

- Petition against crowbar, and midsummer arriving faster than either can win.
- [[The Night Ferry Accord]] — if the rank and file learn their leader bargained secretly with an Ostreve, the word "sworn" cuts the other way.
