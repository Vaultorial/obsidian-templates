# Bren Callow

Net-mender turned orator: the man who asked the Ebb Court for mercy, was refused in four sentences, and built a movement out of the refusal.

## Appearance and manner

Forties, salt-cracked hands he displays deliberately when speaking. No gift for small talk; an alarming gift for crowds. Mends nets while he negotiates — it keeps his voice slow.

## Wants and fears

- Stated want: the Drowning ended, the Lower Ward spared, tide-law amended in daylight.
- Real want: the same — Bren is that rare thing, a leader whose private goal matches his speeches. What he hides is how close he is to losing the dike-breakers' wing of his own oath.
- Fear: that he restrains his people on the strength of [[The Night Ferry Accord]], the vote happens anyway, and he becomes the man who talked Lowmark into drowning quietly.

## Relationships

- [[Verity Ostreve]] — ally by arithmetic, not affection. He keeps her letters as insurance and is ashamed of keeping them.
- [[Perrin Lowsmith]] — quartermaster and conscience; the blight gave Perrin standing with the hard wing that Bren himself lacks.
- [[Elowen Hark]] — the refusal that made him. He studies her rulings the way she studies charts.

## Appears in

- [[The Lowmark Petition]] — the four-sentence refusal, and the oath in the net-shed that night.
- [[The Night Ferry Accord]] — his half of the bargain: no broken dikes before the vote.
- [[The Drowning of Lowmark]] — where every promise he has made comes due at once.
