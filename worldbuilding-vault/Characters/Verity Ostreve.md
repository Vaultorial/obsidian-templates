# Verity Ostreve

Heir of the founding house and the Ebb Court's perfect ceremonial daughter — who has already committed quiet treason and intends to commit more of it.

## Appearance and manner

Late twenties, court-polished, dresses precisely as expected. Listens like a clerk taking minutes. Has cultivated a reputation for harmless dutifulness so consistent that nobody has noticed it is a costume.

## Wants and fears

- Stated want: the dignity of House Ostreve and the smooth working of the Court.
- Real want: an end to the Drowning without the Courts burning — she believes the law can be rewritten if the vote can be delayed past midsummer, and she has been buying time and upland land accordingly.
- Fear: being forced to choose publicly between her name and her accord before either is ready to survive it.

## Relationships

- [[Bren Callow]] — co-conspirator by night-ferry; they do not like each other, which both find reassuring about the bargain.
- [[Wick the Ferryman]] — courier and witness of [[The Night Ferry Accord]]; she trusts his silence and is right to.
- [[Elowen Hark]] — mentor of sorts. Verity counts on the Tidemistress's doubt, which is the riskiest line in her ledger.

## Appears in

- [[The Night Ferry Accord]] — her signature, in a hand she practiced to be deniable.
- [[The Drowning of Lowmark]] — her delay strategy meets its deadline.
