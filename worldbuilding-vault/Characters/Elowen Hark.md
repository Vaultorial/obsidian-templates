# Elowen Hark

The law's living voice — a chart-lawyer who climbed to Tidemistress on pure rigor, now required to enforce a precedent she has begun to doubt.

## Appearance and manner

Sixties, spare, ink-grey robes without ornament; reads standing up. Quotes chart and clause from memory in a voice that never needs raising. Her one tell: when an argument lands, she goes silent and looks at the sea.

## Wants and fears

- Stated want: tide-law upheld exactly, because exactly is the only way law survives at all.
- Real want: to be shown a lawful way out. She refused [[The Lowmark Petition]] on precedent and has counted its cost every season since.
- Fear: that [[Maud Pell]]'s corrections mean the charts — her whole life's instrument — have been quietly wrong, and every ruling with them.

## Relationships

- [[Verity Ostreve]] — protégée in public, problem in private; Elowen suspects the girl is up to something and has chosen, carefully, not to find out what.
- [[Bren Callow]] — the petitioner she refused. She remembers his exact words better than he does.
- [[Maud Pell]] — correspondent of thirty years. The annual chart-letters between them have lately grown very short.

## Appears in

- [[The Lowmark Petition]] — her refusal founded the Floodsworn, a fact she does not dispute.
- [[The Drowning of Lowmark]] — hers is the voice that must order the sluices opened, or not.
