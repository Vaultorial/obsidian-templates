# Wick the Ferryman

Keeper of the night boat between the cities when the causeway floods — and therefore, by accident of profession, the most informed neutral party in the Tide Courts.

## Appearance and manner

Ageless in the way of watermen; could be fifty, could be seventy. Oars without splash, answers without content. His one absolute rule, posted nowhere and known to everyone: what crosses on the boat, stays on the boat.

## Wants and fears

- Stated want: fares, fair weather and no politics aboard.
- Real want: the bay's people intact on both shores. Wick has ferried Ostreve clerks and Lowmark net-haulers for forty years and declines to consider either of them the enemy.
- Fear: that he is now the only bridge left. He carried [[The Night Ferry Accord]]; if it fails, the secret fails with him aboard, and neutrality may stop being a place anyone lets him stand.

## Relationships

- [[Verity Ostreve]] and [[Bren Callow]] — passengers, conspirators, and the two debts of silence he most regrets holding.
- [[Cole Rowe]] — his opposite number on the dry crossing; they trade weather-sense and funerals.
- Everyone else — a fare, eventually. The boat hears it all.

## Appears in

- [[The Night Ferry Accord]] — host and witness; the accord is named after his boat.
- [[The Drowning of Lowmark]] — when the sluices open, his ferry becomes the evacuation's spine.
