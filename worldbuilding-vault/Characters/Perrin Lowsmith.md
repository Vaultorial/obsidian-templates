# Perrin Lowsmith

Third-generation moss-farmer who watched the blight take his family's beds in one season — and learned, in the hungry winter after, exactly how much the Courts' law weighs against an empty grading-hall.

## Appearance and manner

Thirties, built like sledge-hauling, quiet in the way of people who work to tide-clocks. Carries a cured sprig of pearl-moss from the last good harvest and does not explain it. Counts supplies twice; trusts lists over speeches.

## Wants and fears

- Stated want: stores, boats and dry beds for the Lower Ward when the Drowning comes — he is the Floodsworn's quartermaster because somebody has to think in barrels.
- Real want: the eastern beds replanted and his children farming them. Everything political is, for Perrin, a detour back to the moor.
- Fear: that the hard wing is right, that petitions and accords are stalling music, and that he — the practical man — will be the one they ask to plan the dike-breaking.

## Relationships

- [[Bren Callow]] — his orator. Perrin supplies loyalty and logistics, and increasingly the warnings Bren does not want.
- [[Maud Pell]] — unlikely friendship from the blight survey; she measured his dead beds in person, the only official of any stripe who came to look.

## Appears in

- [[The Mirrormoor Blight]] — lost the beds; guided Maud's sampling.
- [[The Drowning of Lowmark]] — his barrels and boats are the difference between an evacuation and a catastrophe.
