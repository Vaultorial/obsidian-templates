# Lowmark

Fish-smoke, stilt-timber and gull-noise: Lowmark stands knee-deep in the bay on ten thousand posts, and its people can read the water the way Ostreve's clerks read ledgers. Twice a day the sea moves through the town's bones; twice a year, at king tide, it tries to keep the place.

## What this place is

The working port of the Tide Courts — the catch lands here, the moss from [[The Mirrormoor]] is graded here, and every cargo that climbs to [[Ostreve]] is wet before it is rich. Nominally ruled by [[The Ebb Court]] under tide-law; actually governed day to day by harbor custom and, increasingly, by the mood of [[The Floodsworn]].

The Lower Ward — the oldest, poorest, most flood-bitten quarter — is the ward scheduled to be deliberately flooded at midsummer in [[The Drowning of Lowmark]], to spare the dike ring that keeps the harbor channel (and Ostreve's undercliff) intact.

## People here

- [[Bren Callow]] — net-mender, orator, leader of the Floodsworn.
- [[Wick the Ferryman]] — keeps the night boat; belongs to Lowmark the way the tide does, loosely and reliably.

## Tensions

- Petition or dike-break: the Floodsworn's two wings pull in different directions, and Bren holds the seam.
- After [[The Mirrormoor Blight]], the Lower Ward went hungry through winter. The Drowning lands this year on people with nothing left to move.

## Connections

- [[The Causeway of Bells]] to Ostreve; moss-boats out to [[The Mirrormoor]]; the upland road to [[Hethwick]] where the Floodsworn print what Lowmark cannot say aloud.
- Site of [[The Night Ferry Accord]] and of the coming [[The Drowning of Lowmark]].
