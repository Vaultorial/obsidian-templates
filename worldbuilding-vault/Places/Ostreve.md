# Ostreve

You smell Ostreve before you climb to it — wax, ink and tar — and you hear it on the hour: the Bell Tower calling the tide-state to everyone below. A city of grey stone stacked up the cliff face, dry-footed and proud of it, looking down on the water that makes it rich.

## What this place is

Seat of [[The Ebb Court]] and capital of the Tide Courts in everything but name. Ostreve holds the master tide-charts, licenses every weir and moss-bed under tide-law, and collects the tolls of [[The Causeway of Bells]]. Its wealth is judicial: the city produces almost nothing and adjudicates almost everything.

House Ostreve founded the city and still provides its ceremonial First Seat — currently the aging patriarch, in practice his granddaughter [[Verity Ostreve]] — but real authority sits with the Tidemistress, [[Elowen Hark]], and her chart-lawyers.

## People here

- [[Elowen Hark]] — Tidemistress, the law's living voice.
- [[Verity Ostreve]] — heir of the founding house, publicly dutiful, privately sworn to [[The Night Ferry Accord]].

## Tensions

- The undercliff dike ring that keeps Ostreve's lower warehouses dry is the very structure [[The Drowning of Lowmark]] protects — the city's comfort is purchased across the bay.
- Chart-lawyers whisper that the Order's last two yearly readings arrived with corrections. Precedent has no procedure for the sea being wrong.

## Connections

- Linked to [[Lowmark]] by [[The Causeway of Bells]] at low water and by [[Wick the Ferryman]]'s night boat otherwise.
- Site of [[The Lowmark Petition]] and of the coming sluice vote.
