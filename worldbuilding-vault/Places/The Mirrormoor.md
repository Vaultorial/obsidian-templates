# The Mirrormoor

At low water it is the largest thing in the world: miles of wet silver flat reflecting the whole sky, crossed by channels that look like cracks in a mirror. The pearl-moss grows in the channel margins — a grey-green nap that cures to iridescence — and the moss-farmers work it at a run, because the sea comes back faster than a laden sledge.

## What this place is

The luxury economy of the Tide Courts. Cured pearl-moss dyes the robes of every court on the Shallow Sea and flavors feast-dishes inland as far as caravans go; the beds are allotted under tide-law, license by license, from [[Ostreve]]. The farmers live on stilt-huts at the moor's edge and grade their harvest in [[Lowmark]].

## People here

- [[Perrin Lowsmith]] — third-generation moss-farmer; lost his family's beds to the blight and found a second vocation as the Floodsworn's quartermaster.

## Tensions

- [[The Mirrormoor Blight]] of 373 killed the eastern beds outright. Licenses say the beds exist; the sea says otherwise; tide-law has no clause for grief.
- [[Maud Pell]]'s blight sampling proved the silt has moved — the finding that quietly threatens every chart the law stands on.

## Connections

- Moss-boats to [[Lowmark]]; the neap-tide footpath to [[The Drowned Abbey]] starts from the moor's northern horn.
- Site of [[The Mirrormoor Blight]].
