# Hesk Adler

Captain of [[The Brine Wardens]] — the road's sworn protector, on the Combine's retainer. The conspiracy's armed wing, and its most dangerous piece because he still believes he is the reasonable one.

## Appearance and manner

Fifties, parade-straight, warden grey kept immaculate. Friendly in the way of a man patting a horse he may have to put down. Gives advice instead of threats; the advice is the threat.

## What he wants

- Stated: order on the road and an end to "bandit panic".
- Real: his pension. The Wardens' toll fund has been thin for years; Carrow coin filled it, and Adler has convinced himself that managed decline under the Combine beats honest collapse. He directs patrols away from the scheme's working parts and inspects what he is told to inspect.

## What he knows

- The false toll crew's schedule and the patrol rotations that protect it.
- That his seal-stamps reopen waystation ledger pages — his clerks do the actual inking.
- The party's movements, reported by at least one informant in Saltmere harbor (DM note: it is a Limpet pot-boy, not [[Joss Brell]]).

## Quick stats

AC 16, HP 52, speed 30 ft. Longsword +6 (1d8+3, two attacks); warden's horn rallies any wardens in earshot. A veteran — fights smart, retreats smarter, and would genuinely rather arrest the party than kill them.
