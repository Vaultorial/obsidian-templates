# Dorrik Vane

The Combine's man in [[Saltmere]]: the conspiracy's bookkeeper, polite to the bone, and the only person who knows where every false number lives.

## Appearance and manner

Forties, soft-spoken, ink-stained cuffs on an expensive coat. Apologizes before delivering bad news and after winning arguments. Counts things when nervous — coins, buttons, the party.

## What he wants

- Stated: the smooth operation of Combine interests on the route.
- Real: to survive his own ledgers. Dorrik did not design the scheme, but he wrote it down, and he knows exactly what happens to clerks when great houses burn their paperwork.

## What he knows

- Both ledgers in the Counting House strongroom: the public accounts and the real ones, with the "road wages" entries that pay [[Hesk Adler]]'s wardens.
- The draft deed of sale for [[The Greyflat Pans]], lot by lot.
- That [[Iselle Carrow]] arriving in person means the timetable has moved up — and that frightens him more than the party does.

## Quick stats

AC 12, HP 18, hidden knife +3 (1d4+1). Won't fight if talking remains available; folds under real pressure if offered protection. The likeliest turncoat in the campaign.

## Appearances

- Referenced in [[Session 2 - The Hollowford Ledger]] and [[Session 3 - Salt in the Wound]]; meets the party on-screen if the [[Counting House Break-In]] goes loud or goes social.
