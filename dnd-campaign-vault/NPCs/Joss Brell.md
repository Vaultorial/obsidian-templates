# Joss Brell

Retired smuggler, corner table at The Limpet, ledger of favors in his head. Saltmere's unofficial registry of everything the Harbor Office cannot ask about.

## Appearance and manner

Big, slow-moving, fingers too scarred for the rings he wears. Buys the second round, never the first. Prices information in favors rather than coin because favors appreciate.

## What he wants

- Stated: a quiet retirement and the occasional profitable conversation.
- Real: the Combine off his docks. A vertically owned harbor has no seams left to smuggle through — Iselle Carrow is bad for independent business, which makes Joss the party's most reliable ally of convenience.

## What he knows

- Who hired the Miller's Cut bravos: a Vellharrow fixer who works a Weighhouse wine room (name available, for a favor).
- Harbor traffic patterns, including which Combine lighters move at night without manifests.
- [[Bram Tuckett]]'s old debt, down to the copper — currently being repaid in errands, which suits Joss better than money ever would.

## Quick stats

AC 12, HP 30, dockhand's hook +4 (1d6+2). Two old crewmates drink within whistling distance whenever he talks business.
