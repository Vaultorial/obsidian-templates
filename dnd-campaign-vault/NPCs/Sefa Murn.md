# Sefa Murn

Steward of the salt-harvesters' brotherhood: the person the Combine must move, buy or break to get [[The Greyflat Pans]] — and the campaign's measure of whether the party's victory is clean or bloody.

## Appearance and manner

Fifties, salt-burned forearms, voice pitched to carry over wind. Hospitality first, business second, grudges kept like charters: in writing, in a sea chest. Since the barrows walked, a new flatness in her voice.

## What she wants

- Stated: the road safe, the salt moving, the Combine's "offer" answered with a closed gate.
- Real: after the desecration — names. The pansmen have buried their own on that dune line for generations; coin was negotiable, the barrows are not.

## What she knows

- The brotherhood's charters prove common title to the pans — the sale requires her seal or her removal.
- The warden post log copies (old custom: the post shares its log with the steward), showing patrols redirected on captain's orders.
- Which pansmen are frightened enough to sell, and which are angry enough to march on Saltmere if she lets them.

## Quick stats

AC 12, HP 16, pan-hook +3 (1d6+1). Fights only in defense of the pans, and then without ceremony.

## Appearances

- [[Session 3 - Salt in the Wound]] — received the party, survived the saltwight night, became an ally with conditions.
