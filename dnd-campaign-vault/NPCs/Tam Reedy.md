# Tam Reedy

The drover who came back. The only living witness to how the wagons disappear — currently sleeping in a shrine cellar and jumping at cart wheels.

## Appearance and manner

Twenties, wiry, hands that miss having reins in them. Talks fast when he finally talks, then stops mid-sentence and listens to the road. Loyal to whoever proves they can keep him alive.

## What he wants

- Stated: to get out of the Brinemark and never see salt again.
- Real: to stop being afraid. If the party shows him the conspiracy actually falling, he will testify — he liked his wagon, his route and his life, and someone took them.

## What he knows

- The false toll stop: cloaked men, Warden grey under the cloaks, a practiced routine — his was not the first wagon they took.
- The hidden fork at Miller's Cut and the repainting camp inside it.
- The face of the marsh-knife sergeant the party killed in [[Session 1 - Blood on the Brine]] — he can tie the ambushers to the false toll crew.

## Quick stats

AC 11, HP 7, no stomach for a fight; superb wagon handling if escape happens on wheels.
