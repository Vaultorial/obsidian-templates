# Brother Edlin

Keeper of the lantern shrine at [[Hollowford Waystation]] — a small, round, unhurried man who has decided, without drama, to hide a hunted witness under a holy floor.

## Appearance and manner

Sixty-odd, bald as an egg, robe patched with sailcloth. Trims the lamp mid-conversation. Asks questions like a man filling a ledger of souls: gently, and remembering every answer.

## What he wants

- Stated: nothing. The lamp stays lit, travelers stay fed.
- Real: for [[Tam Reedy]] to be someone else's burden before the festival fills his yard with strangers — and for the road to go back to being merely dangerous, instead of owned.

## What he knows

- Forty years of waystation gossip, which is to say: the road's entire social history.
- Which Warden patrols pray and which patrols only water their horses — he marked the change in the rotation six weeks before anyone else did.
- The shrine cellar is charter-sacred: even Wardens need a magistrate's writ to search it. He is quietly daring them to try.

## Quick stats

Non-combatant. AC 10, HP 6, and the moral authority to stop a sword with a raised eyebrow, once, per the DM's judgment.
