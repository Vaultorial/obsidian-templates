# Maera Wace

The harbormaster of [[Saltmere]], and the only official in the Brinemark who both sees the whole problem and is honest enough to pay someone to dig at it.

## Appearance and manner

Late fifties, weathered like a pier post, dock-keeper's coat older than the party. Talks in cargo terms: people are "sound hulls" or "rot below the waterline". Never raises her voice; lowers it instead.

## What she wants

- Stated: find out why the road is bleeding wagons before the insurers kill the route for good.
- Real, admitted in session three's letter: she suspects the rot reaches people she cannot touch, and wants proof solid enough that touching them becomes someone else's duty.

## What she knows

- Every cargo, owner and insurance claim through Saltmere for thirty years.
- That [[Dorrik Vane]]'s manifest copies never quite match her own — she noticed before she hired anyone, and said nothing until she trusted the party.
- Nothing about [[Hesk Adler]]'s corruption yet; the Wardens were her idea once, and it will land hard.

## Quick stats

Non-combatant. If cornered: AC 11, HP 9, belaying pin +1 (1d4). Her weapon is the writ ledger.
