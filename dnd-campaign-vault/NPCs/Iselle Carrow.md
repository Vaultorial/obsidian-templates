# Iselle Carrow

Lady of Carrow Hall, head of [[The Carrow Combine]], and the author of the Salt Road conspiracy. Currently moored in [[Saltmere]] harbor aboard her barge, being publicly helpful.

## Appearance and manner

Sixties, silver-haired, dresses a careful half-step below her wealth. Remembers every name and uses them warmly. Speaks of the road's troubles with genuine-sounding sorrow — she has been rehearsing sincerity for forty years.

## What she wants

- Stated: "to restore confidence in the road my house was built on."
- Real: the pans bought, the route's independents broken, and the whole Brinemark trade vertically hers. The wagons and the fear were simply the cheapest tools available.

## What she knows

- Everything, in outline; nothing, on paper. Iselle keeps her distance from the mechanics — that is what factors and captains are for.
- She does not know about [[Tam Reedy]], and does not yet take the party seriously. Both facts have expiry dates.

## Quick stats

Non-combatant, never alone: two house guards (AC 14, HP 16, sword +4, 1d8+2) within call at all times. Her real defenses are lawyers, magistrates and the burden of proof.
