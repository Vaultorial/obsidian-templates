# Vellharrow

Four days inland, where the hills open into market country: a walled city of weighhouses, counting rooms and guild banners, rich on everything the coast produces and convinced it earned all of it.

## What this place is

The landward end of [[The Salt Road]] and the place where salt becomes money. Prices are called from the Weighhouse steps each morning; insurers, brokers and magistrates do the rest. [[The Carrow Combine]] was born here and Carrow Hall still dominates the merchants' quarter.

## Notable places

- **Carrow Hall** — seat of [[Iselle Carrow]]'s house: part palace, part counting floor.
- **The Weighhouse** — where the route's "danger" gets priced; the insurers' refusals all trace back to whispers that started on these steps.
- **The Magistrates' Court** — the campaign's legal endgame, if the party chooses exposure over rougher justice.
