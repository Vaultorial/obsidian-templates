# Saltmere

Gulls, tar, and the white dust that gets into everything. Saltmere is a working harbor town that smells of brine and woodsmoke, where every third building is a warehouse and the tide bell rules the day.

## What this place is

The seaward end of [[The Salt Road]] and the only deep anchorage on this stretch of coast. Salt from [[The Greyflat Pans]] is weighed, taxed and loaded here. The town answers to a harbor council, but in practice [[Maera Wace]]'s Harbor Office runs everything that floats or rolls.

## Notable places

- **The Harbor Office** — Maera's domain; the writ of passage came from this desk.
- **The Counting House** — the Combine's local office, all polished brass and clerks. [[Dorrik Vane]] keeps the route's books here. Target of the [[Counting House Break-In]].
- **The Limpet** — dockside tavern where [[Joss Brell]] holds court at the corner table and debts are remembered precisely.

## People here

- [[Maera Wace]] — harbormaster, the party's patron.
- [[Dorrik Vane]] — Combine factor, courteous and watchful.
- [[Joss Brell]] — smuggler turned information broker.
- [[Iselle Carrow]] — in residence aboard her barge since mid-May.

## Hooks and rumors

- "The insurers won't write the road anymore — Maera's finished by autumn." (True in spirit; the Combine is feeding it.)
- "The Counting House burns lamps all night since her ladyship docked." (True — see the break-in prep.)
- "Tam Reedy ran off with his cargo and a dockside girl." (False; Combine-planted slander.)

## Secrets

- Dorrik Vane's strongroom holds two ledgers: the public one and the route's real accounts, including payments coded "road wages" that match Warden pay periods.
