# The Greyflat Pans

A mirror-world at low tide: shallow rectangles of seawater going silver and then white as they dry, windbreaks of driftwood, and cottages standing on stilts above the flood line. The pans have fed Saltmere for as long as either has existed.

## What this place is

The source of the wealth everyone else is fighting over. The pansmen — the salt-harvesters' brotherhood — hold the pans in common under tide-old custom, steered by their steward [[Sefa Murn]]. Their salt travels [[The Salt Road]] or it travels nowhere, which is the lever [[The Carrow Combine]] is leaning on.

## Notable places

- **The steward's cottage** — Sefa's; the brotherhood's charters and the warden post log copies live in a sea chest.
- **The old barrows** — grave-mounds on the dune line, tended for generations, recently desecrated with dumped brine waste. Source of the [[Saltwights on the Flats]].
- **The warden post** — a stilt tower with a view of road and flats; its patrols were ordered elsewhere six weeks ago.

## People here

- [[Sefa Murn]] — steward of the pansmen; ally since [[Session 3 - Salt in the Wound]].
- Edda Thorne — [[Kestrel Thorne]]'s cousin, a pans worker; useful for messages, kept off-screen so far.
