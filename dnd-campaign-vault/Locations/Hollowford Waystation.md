# Hollowford Waystation

A walled wagon-yard at the road's halfway ford: stables, a smithy, a deep sweet-water well, and a little shrine whose lantern never goes out. Everything on the Salt Road stops here, which is exactly why it matters.

## What this place is

The neutral ground of [[The Salt Road]]. By old charter the waystation logs every wagon, weight and seal that passes — its ledger is the road's memory, and in quiet ways its conscience. The yardmaster rents beds and stabling; the shrine takes care of the rest.

## Notable places

- **The ledger room** — bound volumes going back decades. Stamped pages can only be reopened under Warden seal, which is the heart of the manifest problem found in [[Session 2 - The Hollowford Ledger]].
- **The shrine of the Lantern Saint** — one room, one lamp, [[Brother Edlin]]. The cellar under it is not on any plan.
- **The smithy** — the farrier who unknowingly re-shod the stolen wagons.

## People here

- [[Brother Edlin]] — shrine keeper; currently hiding [[Tam Reedy]] in the cellar.
- [[Tam Reedy]] — the road's only living witness, going quietly mad with waiting.

## Hooks and rumors

- "The Wardens inspect harder than they used to, but the road's never been worse." (True, and the contradiction is the clue.)
- "Edlin feeds two from a pot that should feed one." (True; observant guests notice.)

## Secrets

- The ledger's altered pages all follow inspections signed by patrols under [[Hesk Adler]]'s direct command — nobody has cross-checked the signatures yet.
