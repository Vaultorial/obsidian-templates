# Ambush at Miller's Cut

Run in [[Session 1 - Blood on the Brine]]. Kept for the record and because the sergeant's gear became evidence.

## Setup

Miller's Cut: a sunken stretch of road between scrub banks, an "abandoned" cart angled across it as bait. The crew's job is not robbery — it is discouraging anyone who looks at the cut too long.

## Opposition

Original stat lines for this campaign:

- **Combine bravo** (x4) — AC 12, HP 9, speed 30 ft. Club +3 (1d6+1). Cheap muscle: breaks when the fight turns or the sergeant drops.
- **Marsh-knife sergeant** — AC 14, HP 22, speed 30 ft. Shortsword +4 (1d6+2, two attacks); *Snake the gap*: once per fight, moves through enemy reach without provoking. Fights to the end — paid too well to run, too professional to surrender.

## Terrain and complications

- Banks give the bravos high ground and half cover at the start.
- The bait cart can be shoved into combat as a one-time crush hazard (DC 12 to dodge clear, 2d6 if caught).

## Tactics

- Bravos open with thrown stones from the banks; sergeant waits until someone climbs.
- They aim to bloody and frighten, not kill — until the sergeant judges the party as hired investigators, at which point it becomes silencing work.

## Outcomes

- As played: sergeant dead, two bravos captured, two fled toward Vellharrow. The brass salt-grain token entered evidence; the fleeing pair means the Combine knows snoops are working the road.
