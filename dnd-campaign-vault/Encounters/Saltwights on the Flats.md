# Saltwights on the Flats

Run in [[Session 3 - Salt in the Wound]]. The campaign's one supernatural night — written so the horror lands on the Combine, not on the dead.

## Setup

The old barrows on the dune line, desecrated by dumped pan-brine. On a moonless tide, the buried dead rise crusted in salt and walk toward the nearest living warmth: the pansmen's cottages.

## Opposition

Original creatures for this campaign:

- **Saltwight** (x3) — AC 13, HP 26, speed 20 ft. Crusted claw +4 (1d8+2); *Parching grasp*: a creature it hits cannot regain hit points until the end of its next turn; *Brine-born*: unharmed by the channels' cold water; salt crust crumbles under blunt force (bludgeoning damage breaks its AC to 11 until its turn ends).

## Terrain and complications

- Brine channels crosshatch the flats: difficult ground for the party, ignored by the wights.
- The tide is coming in. Ten rounds after the fight starts, the channels become swimming obstacles and the cottages' stilts start to matter.
- Lantern light draws them; dousing lights redirects wights but leaves the party fighting in the dark.

## Tactics

- Wights are slow, relentless and stupid: straight lines toward warmth and light. The fight is about positioning, the tide clock and protecting the cottages — not about outfighting them.

## Outcomes

- As played: all three destroyed, one cottage porch lost to fire, no pansmen dead. [[Yneva Marsh]] recovered a grave-torc and wants the barrows resealed with proper rites — doing so will bind the remaining dead and earn the pansmen's lasting trust; leaving it invites a repeat on the next moonless tide.
