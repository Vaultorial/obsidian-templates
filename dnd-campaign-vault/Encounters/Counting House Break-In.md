# Counting House Break-In

Prepped for the next session. The party voted to go in quiet and take the real ledgers from [[Dorrik Vane]]'s strongroom. An infiltration, not a battle — but it has teeth if it goes loud.

## Setup

The Counting House: stone ground floor, clerk floor above, strongroom at the back of the ground floor. Since [[Iselle Carrow]] docked, lamps burn late and the watch is doubled. Best window: the harbor festival night, when the street noise covers and half the watch is borrowed for the procession.

## Opposition

- **House watchman** (x4, two per floor in rotation) — AC 13, HP 11, speed 30 ft. Cudgel +3 (1d6+1); blows a whistle before fighting if given the chance.
- **Strongroom door** — iron-banded, quality lock (DC 15 with tools; DC 12 if the party obtained the clerk's spare key through [[Joss Brell]]'s favor network).
- **Alarm bell** — rope behind the clerks' desks; any watchman who reaches it brings a Warden patrol in five rounds, and [[Hesk Adler]] personally if the festival has him in town.

## What is in the strongroom

- The second ledger: "road wages" entries matching Warden pay periods — the proof that buys [[Maera Wace]] her magistrates.
- The draft deed of sale for [[The Greyflat Pans]], lots matching the surveyor stakes.
- A locked letter-case with the Carrow seal: correspondence Iselle believes destroyed (DM: contents decided by what the story needs her to have written down).

## Tactics

- Watchmen fight to raise the alarm, not to win. The encounter's real clock is noise.
- If captured, the party faces Saltmere law with Combine lawyers attached — a story turn, not a dead end.

## Outcomes

- Clean theft: the conspiracy is provable and the endgame moves to Vellharrow's magistrates — or to Sefa's harder way.
- Loud theft: proof in hand but the party hunted, and Adler finally has the legal pretext he wanted.
- Failure: Dorrik Vane knows exactly who came for his books, and what that means for his own neck — he may defect to the party first.
