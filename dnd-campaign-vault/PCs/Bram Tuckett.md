# Bram Tuckett

"I'm reformed. Reformed people read other people's ledgers all the time." Ex-smuggler working off an old debt to [[Joss Brell]], one errand at a time.

## Bonds and hooks

- The debt to [[Joss Brell]] — never named aloud in coin, repaid in favors that keep getting larger. Joss's tips have been good so far; the day one is not, Bram will have a decision to make.
- Knows [[Saltmere]]'s docks the way clerks know desks: which warehouses have soft locks, which watchmen take naps, which lighters move at night.

## Goals

- Character: clear the debt and open a legitimate chandlery — he has the shopfront picked out and will not say where.
- Player (Alex): heists and social infiltration; has been openly lobbying for the [[Counting House Break-In]] since session two.

## Table notes

- AC 14, passive perception 13; the careful hand that copied the Hollowford ledger pages.
- Will not steal from the shrine, the pansmen or anyone who feeds him. Everything else is negotiable.
