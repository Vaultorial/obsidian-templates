# Yneva Marsh

"A lit road is a kept promise." Cleric of the Lantern Saint, sent to tend the road shrines of the Brinemark — and arriving to find the road itself being strangled.

## Bonds and hooks

- Her order keeps the waystation shrines; [[Brother Edlin]] is effectively her senior in everything but rank, and his trust in her unlocked [[Tam Reedy]].
- Carries the blessing of the road from [[Session 2 - The Hollowford Ledger]] and, since session three, a saltwight's grave-torc she has sworn to rebury with proper rites at [[The Greyflat Pans]].

## Goals

- Character: reseal the barrows, keep Tam alive, and see the road kept by people worthy of it — she has not decided whether Adler can be redeemed, which the DM intends to make expensive either way.
- Player (Priya): moral weight and ritual scenes; asked for "consequences with names attached".

## Table notes

- AC 16, passive perception 14; the lantern rites in the saltwight fight were her idea, not a spell list entry — reward that kind of play.
- Quietly keeps the party's accounts honest: Maera's coin, Joss's favors, who owes whom.

## Session log

- [[Session 1 - Blood on the Brine]] — insisted the dead sergeant be buried, which the prisoners noticed.
- [[Session 2 - The Hollowford Ledger]] — won Edlin's trust; received the blessing of the road.
- [[Session 3 - Salt in the Wound]] — turned the saltwight night; took up the grave-torc duty.
