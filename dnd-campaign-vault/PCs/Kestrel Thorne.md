# Kestrel Thorne

"Roads remember everything, if you walk them slow enough." Tracker and former toll-path guide; the party's eyes on [[The Salt Road]].

## Bonds and hooks

- Cousin **Edda Thorne** works [[The Greyflat Pans]] — family duty pulled Kestrel into the pansmen's side of the story, and got the party through [[Sefa Murn]]'s door.
- Walked warden patrol routes as a hired guide years ago; respects what [[The Brine Wardens]] were, which makes [[Hesk Adler]]'s corruption personal.

## Goals

- Character: prove the road can belong to the people who walk it, not the people who price it.
- Player (Dana): more wilderness problem-solving; loved the tracking scenes at Miller's Cut.

## Table notes

- AC 14, passive perception 16 — checks the back trail at every camp without being asked.
- Speaks the pans dialect badly; Edda finds this funnier than Kestrel does.

- [[Session 2 - The Hollowford Ledger]] — clocked Adler's patrol arriving with clean boots after "a night ride".
- [[Session 3 - Salt in the Wound]] — found the surveyor stakes; promised Edda the pans would not be sold.
