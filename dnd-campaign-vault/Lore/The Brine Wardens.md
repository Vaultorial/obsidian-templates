# The Brine Wardens

The road's law: grey-cloaked riders sworn to keep [[The Salt Road]] passable, funded by the Hollowford toll, answerable to a charter older than any house in [[Vellharrow]]. Once the Brinemark's proudest institution. Currently its saddest.

## What they were

Founded after a bandit generation nearly killed the road, the Wardens were built to be unbuyable: paid from the toll trust, forbidden private retainers, their seals — which can reopen a stamped waybill — entrusted only to captains. Drovers' children still wave at the grey cloaks. Most wardens still deserve it.

## What they are now

Thin. Traffic fell, the toll fund fell with it, and wages went stale years ago. When Carrow money quietly topped up the fund, Captain [[Hesk Adler]] chose to call it patronage instead of purchase. What the Combine actually bought:

- Patrol routes that avoid Miller's Cut on the right nights and the barrows at [[The Greyflat Pans]] always.
- Captain's seals reopening stamped ledger pages at [[Hollowford Waystation]] for [[Dorrik Vane]]'s corrections.
- A captain who arrives anywhere the investigation goes, friendly and exquisitely informed — see [[Session 2 - The Hollowford Ledger]].

## What the rank and file know

Almost nothing, and that is the campaign's live wire. The ordinary wardens believe the rerouted patrols are about staffing. A party that exposes Adler carefully inherits an honest armed force; a party that humiliates the Wardens wholesale leaves the road with no law at all — which suits [[Iselle Carrow]] fine, as it happens.

## Table reference

- Patrols ride in fours; sergeants carry horn and writ. Ordinary warden: AC 14, HP 13, spear +3 (1d6+1) — original numbers for this campaign.
- The patrol log copies held by [[Sefa Murn]] are admissible before Vellharrow magistrates if a serving warden vouches for them. Finding that warden is an adventure seed.
