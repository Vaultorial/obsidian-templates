# Session 1 - Blood on the Brine

## Recap

Three wagons have vanished on the Salt Road in two months, the latest with drover Tam Reedy aboard. Harbormaster [[Maera Wace]] hired the party to find out why the road is bleeding. At Miller's Cut they were ambushed by bandits who fought like paid soldiers, and found a brass token stamped with a salt grain — the mark of [[The Carrow Combine]].

## What happened

- Opened in [[Saltmere]]. Maera laid it out in the Harbor Office: wagons gone without wreckage, drovers gone without bodies, and the Vellharrow insurers now refusing the route. She offered fair coin and a writ of passage.
- [[Joss Brell]] cornered [[Bram Tuckett]] at The Limpet with a tip, half-paid in advance forgiveness of Bram's old debt: "Bandits that let the toll coach pass untouched are not bandits. Someone is choosing the wagons."
- The party rode out to Miller's Cut, where the last wagon's tracks end. [[Kestrel Thorne]] found wheel ruts turning into the brush — into the cut, not off the road.
- Ambush: four bravos and a marsh-knife sergeant ([[Ambush at Miller's Cut]]). The sergeant fought to the last; the bravos broke and two were taken alive.
- The prisoners knew nothing useful — hired in Vellharrow by a fixer, paid in good coin, told to watch the cut and discourage snoops. One carried the brass salt-grain token.
- In the hidden fork of the cut: paint flecks in two colors, fresh wood shavings, and a farrier's rasp. Wagons are being repainted, not destroyed.

## Clues and information handed out

- The brass salt-grain token (Combine men carry them as tavern credit).
- Wagons leave the road at Miller's Cut and come out looking like different wagons.
- The "bandits" are paid from Vellharrow and avoid Warden patrols suspiciously well.

## NPCs met or changed

- [[Maera Wace]] — patron, increasingly out of her depth.
- [[Joss Brell]] — informant, wants Bram's debt repaid in favors.

## Loot and rewards

- Advance from Maera, the brass token, a good riding map of [[The Salt Road]].

## Next time

- Follow the rasp: the nearest farrier on the road works at [[Hollowford Waystation]].
- Decide what to do with the two prisoners before Warden patrol questions come up.
