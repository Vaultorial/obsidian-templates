# Session 3 - Salt in the Wound

## Recap

On [[The Greyflat Pans]] the party heard the other half of the scheme: with the road "too dangerous", [[The Carrow Combine]] is offering to buy the pans at a fraction of their worth. That night the old barrows gave up their dead — saltwights, stirred by brine waste the Combine's surveyors dumped over the graves. After the fight, the party found surveyor stakes already marking the pans into Combine lots, and learned the Wardens were ordered to patrol away from the barrows. The session ended with word from Saltmere: Lady [[Iselle Carrow]] has arrived in person, to "restore confidence in the road".

## What happened

- [[Sefa Murn]] received the party on the strength of [[Kestrel Thorne]]'s cousin Edda. The pansmen's position: harvests are good, but with insurers refusing the route their salt sits unsold. A Combine factor's purchase offer arrived a week ago — insultingly low, politely worded, second notice already.
- Kestrel surveyed the dune line and found fresh-cut stakes with the salt-grain mark, plotting the pans into numbered lots. The Combine is dividing the spoils before the sale.
- Night watch: three saltwights came up out of the old barrows and made for the cottages ([[Saltwights on the Flats]]). Hard fight in the brine channels; [[Yneva Marsh]]'s lantern rites turned the worst of it.
- After the fight, the party found the barrow ditch wet with dumped pan-brine — the surveyors' waste, poured over graves the pansmen have tended for generations. Sefa, white with anger: "They didn't even know what they were waking. They just didn't want to cart it."
- A warden post stands within sight of the barrows. Its log (Sefa holds a copy by old custom) shows patrols redirected to the coast track six weeks ago, on captain's orders.
- A runner from Maera reached the party at dawn: Lady Iselle Carrow's barge docked in Saltmere, and the Counting House windows have been lit all night.

## Clues and information handed out

- The purchase offers and the surveyor stakes: the motive, in writing.
- Patrol redirection on [[Hesk Adler]]'s order — the corruption now has a name.
- The saltwights tie the Combine to desecration, which the pansmen will fight over even if coin will not move them.

## NPCs met or changed

- [[Sefa Murn]] — ally; will rouse the pansmen if the party brings proof that names the Combine.
- [[Hesk Adler]] — implicated by the patrol log.
- [[Iselle Carrow]] — arrived in Saltmere; not yet met.

## Loot and rewards

- A saltwight's grave-torc of crusted silver (Yneva wants it reburied properly), Sefa's copy of the warden post log.
