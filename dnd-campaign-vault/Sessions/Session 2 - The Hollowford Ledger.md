# Session 2 - The Hollowford Ledger

## Recap

At [[Hollowford Waystation]] the party found the missing drover alive: [[Brother Edlin]] has been hiding [[Tam Reedy]] in the shrine cellar. Tam saw his wagon driven into the cut and repainted in Combine colors. The waystation ledger shows manifests altered after Warden inspection — which should be impossible without Warden seals. Then Captain [[Hesk Adler]] arrived, smiling, and advised everyone to let the matter rest.

## What happened

- The waystation farrier confirmed the rasp was his, sold "to a Combine teamster" along with re-shoeing for wagons he was paid not to log.
- [[Yneva Marsh]] recognized the shrine's lantern-keeping and got [[Brother Edlin]] talking. Edlin admitted hiding a frightened man "from the road's new owners" — Tam Reedy.
- Tam's testimony: stopped at a false toll, crew bag over his head, wagon driven into the fork at Miller's Cut. He slipped his ropes and ran across the flats at night. He will not testify while the Wardens patrol the road: "The men at the false toll wore Warden grey under their cloaks."
- [[Bram Tuckett]] spent the night with the waystation ledger. Three manifests show weights and owners amended in a different ink, each one after the Warden inspection stamp — only Warden seals reopen a stamped page.
- Captain [[Hesk Adler]] arrived with a patrol at dawn, polite and well-informed about the party's movements. He called the ambush "settled banditry", offered escort back to Saltmere, and suggested Maera's coin would be better spent on toll subsidies.
- The party declined the escort and let Adler believe they were heading home.

## Clues and information handed out

- Tam Reedy's full account (the campaign's first willing witness, if he can be made safe).
- The doctored manifests: whoever edits them holds Warden seals.
- Adler knew details nobody outside the investigation should have.

## NPCs met or changed

- [[Tam Reedy]] — status changed from missing to alive (in hiding).
- [[Brother Edlin]] — quiet ally; will keep hiding Tam but wants him gone before festival traffic.
- [[Hesk Adler]] — introduced; suspicion planted.

## Loot and rewards

- Copied ledger pages (Bram's careful hand), Edlin's blessing of the road for Yneva.
