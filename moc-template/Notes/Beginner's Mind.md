# Beginner's Mind

Up: [[Learning MOC]]

The posture of approaching material as if you did not already know it — asking the obvious question, taking the basic course, letting yourself be visibly wrong. Expertise creates a subtle tax: protecting the *image* of knowing starts to compete with knowing, and the questions that would help most become the ones too embarrassing to ask.

Every technique in this map quietly bills to this account. [[Active Recall]] only works if failed recalls are information rather than injury; the [[Feynman Technique]] only works if a stalled explanation triggers curiosity instead of cover-up. The practices are mechanical; the willingness to look ignorant is the fuel.

Related: [[Freewriting]] in the [[Writing MOC]] is beginner's mind applied to drafting — permission to produce bad sentences on the way to true ones.
