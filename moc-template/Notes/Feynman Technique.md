# Feynman Technique

Up: [[Learning MOC]]

Explain the idea in plain words, as if to a bright newcomer; where the explanation stalls, hedges, or reaches for jargon, you have located the part you do not actually understand. Then return to the source for *that part specifically* and try again. The technique works because explanation refuses the shortcuts that recognition allows — you cannot hand-wave to an imagined listener who keeps asking "but why?".

The written version is even stricter: a paragraph in your own words, no peeking. It doubles as a permanent note for the vault, which is the quiet overlap between learning practice and note-making.

Related: [[Active Recall]] is the same mechanism at sentence scale; [[Writing for One Reader]] in the [[Writing MOC]] aims the explanation at a real person; [[Beginner's Mind]] makes the stalls survivable.
