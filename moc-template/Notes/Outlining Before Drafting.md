# Outlining Before Drafting

Up: [[Writing MOC]]

Settle the argument before writing the sentences. An outline is cheap to rearrange — moving a bullet costs nothing, moving a finished section costs an afternoon and some grief. The pieces that collapse in revision usually had a structural flaw that ten minutes of outlining would have exposed: a missing step, two sections doing the same job, a conclusion the middle never earns.

An outline is claims in order, not topics: "readers skim because the stakes arrive too late", not "introduction". If a bullet cannot be phrased as a claim, that part is not ready to draft.

Related: [[Freewriting]] serves the pieces that resist outlining — discover first, structure after; [[Editing in Passes]] starts with a structural pass, which is just outlining a draft that already exists.
