# Time Blocking

Up: [[Productivity MOC]]

Give every working hour a job in advance: the calendar stops being a list of meetings and becomes a plan for attention. The point is not rigidity — blocks get moved constantly — it is that *moving* a block is a conscious trade, while an unplanned hour disappears without ever being decided about.

Two rules keep it honest. First, block the deep work before anything else claims the space; shallow tasks will happily fill whatever remains (pair with [[Task Batching]]). Second, schedule against energy, not just availability — a deep block at the day's low tide is fiction (see [[Energy Management]]).

Related: [[Weekly Review]] is where next week's blocks get drafted; [[Daily Writing Habit]] is one block defended long enough to become identity.
