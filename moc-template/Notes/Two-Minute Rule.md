# Two-Minute Rule

Up: [[Productivity MOC]]

Two uses, same name. As a processing rule: if a task takes under two minutes, do it the moment you meet it — tracking it would cost more than doing it. As a starting trick: when a task feels too big to begin, commit to only its first two minutes; the resistance lives almost entirely at the threshold, and a started task has different physics than an unstarted one.

The trap is letting two-minute tasks interrupt deep work — the rule applies when *processing* an inbox, not when something pings mid-block. Caught mid-focus, the small task goes to a list and waits for a batch.

Related: [[Task Batching]] is where deferred small tasks go to die efficiently; [[Weekly Review]] relies on the processing form of the rule to stay under an hour.
