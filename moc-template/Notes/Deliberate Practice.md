# Deliberate Practice

Up: [[Learning MOC]]

Repetition improves a skill only up to the point of comfort; past that, hours stop counting. Deliberate practice is the alternative: isolate the weakest component, work on it specifically at the edge of current ability, get fast feedback, repeat. It is targeted, effortful, and slightly unpleasant — a session that felt smooth was probably performance, not practice.

The design questions for any session: what exactly am I trying to improve, how will I know within minutes whether it worked, and is this hard enough that failure is happening regularly? No to any of these means the session is rehearsal.
