# Writing for One Reader

Up: [[Writing MOC]]

Prose aimed at everyone lands on no one. Picking a single concrete reader — a colleague, a friend, yourself two years ago — makes a hundred decisions automatically: what needs explaining, which examples will land, where patience runs out, what can go unsaid. "The general audience" answers none of these, so the writing hedges, over-explains, and flattens into committee voice.

The chosen reader also functions as a test instrument: read the draft as them and the weak spots announce themselves — here they would skim, here they would ask "so what?", here they would not believe you.

Related: [[Feynman Technique]] in the [[Learning MOC]] uses an imagined listener the same way, as a detector; [[Editing in Passes]] is where the one-reader test gets applied systematically.
