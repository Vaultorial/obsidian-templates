# Freewriting

Up: [[Writing MOC]]

Set a timer, write continuously, and do not stop — no deleting, no rereading, no fixing. The rule sounds gimmicky and is actually structural: generation and judgment are different modes, and trying to run both at once is how a writer ends up polishing sentence three for an hour. Freewriting forcibly unplugs the judge so the generator can find out what it thinks.

The output is ore, not metal. Most of it gets discarded; the working session that follows mines it for the two surprising sentences that survive. Ten minutes is enough to break a stall on almost anything.

Related: [[Editing in Passes]] is where judgment gets its turn, later and on purpose; [[Beginner's Mind]] in the [[Learning MOC]] is the same permission granted to thinking; [[Outlining Before Drafting]] is the opposite door into a draft — some pieces need one, some the other.
