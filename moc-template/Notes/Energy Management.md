# Energy Management

Up: [[Productivity MOC]]

Hours are interchangeable on a calendar and wildly unequal in real life. An hour of sharp morning attention and a foggy hour after a long meeting are different currencies, and most planning failures are category errors between them. Managing energy means noticing your own pattern — when the good hours reliably occur — and assigning work by kind: creation in the peaks, administration in the troughs, recovery on purpose rather than by collapse.

Track it crudely for two weeks (a number from one to five, three times a day, in a daily note) and the pattern is usually undeniable. Then let the pattern, not optimism, draft the schedule.

Related: [[Time Blocking]] is the tool this note aims; [[Deliberate Practice]] is the most energy-expensive work there is, and the first thing to schedule into a peak.
