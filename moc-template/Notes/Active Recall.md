# Active Recall

Up: [[Learning MOC]]

Close the book and produce the answer from memory — that act, not the rereading before it, is what builds recall. Recognition is a comfortable impostor: notes look familiar, so the material feels known, and the feeling collapses the first time nothing is there to recognize against. Recall practice is uncomfortable for exactly the reason it works: it rehearses the retrieval you will actually need.

Lightweight forms: write what you remember before reopening a source; turn headings into questions and answer them cold; explain yesterday's material to nobody over coffee. None require an app.

Related: [[Spaced Repetition]] schedules the recall attempts; [[Feynman Technique]] is recall stretched into full explanation; [[Beginner's Mind]] supplies the tolerance for being wrong mid-practice.
