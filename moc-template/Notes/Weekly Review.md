# Weekly Review

Up: [[Productivity MOC]]

A standing appointment with your own systems: empty the inboxes, look at every open commitment, decide what next week is actually for. Without it, every other technique decays quietly — blocks stop matching reality, lists grow stale, and the trusted system stops being trusted, which is the moment the head starts keeping its own anxious copy.

Keep it small enough to survive: forty-five minutes, same time weekly, three questions — what happened, what is open, what matters next week. The output is a handful of decisions and next week's calendar blocks ([[Time Blocking]]), not a beautiful report.

Related: [[Two-Minute Rule]] keeps the inboxes shallow enough that the review stays short; [[Energy Management]] supplies the honest question "what did this week cost?".
