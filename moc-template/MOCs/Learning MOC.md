# Learning MOC

Up: [[Home MOC]]

Notes on retaining and understanding, arranged as a pipeline: get it in, keep it in, deepen it, and hold the right posture throughout.

## Keeping it in

- [[Active Recall]] — test yourself instead of rereading; the single highest-leverage switch
- [[Spaced Repetition]] — return to material on a widening schedule, just as it starts to fade

## Deepening it

- [[Feynman Technique]] — explain it simply enough to expose what you don't actually know
- [[Deliberate Practice]] — targeted work at the edge of ability, not comfortable repetition

## Posture

- [[Beginner's Mind]] — the willingness to look ignorant that all of the above quietly require
