# Start Here

Welcome. This vault tracks habits two ways — pick the one that matches your patience, or run both like the examples do.

- **The simple way:** one note per month with a grid — days down, habits across, `✓` or `·` in each cell. No plugins, fully usable on paper-brain days.
- **The advanced way:** one tiny note per day with the habits as true/false frontmatter, and a [[Habit Dashboard]] that computes streaks, monthly totals and perfect days with Dataview.

Two fully tracked months are included (April and May 2026, five everyday habits: read 20 min, walk 30 min, no sugar, lights out by 23:30, journal). The data is deliberately imperfect — a crunch week, weekend slumps, a holiday dent — because that is what real tracking looks like, and both views show the same story.

## How the vault is organized

- `Habits/2026-04 Habits.md`, `Habits/2026-05 Habits.md` — the monthly grids, totals at the bottom.
- `Habits/Daily Log/` — 61 per-day log notes feeding the dashboard.
- `Habits/Habit Dashboard.md` — streaks, month-by-month totals, last 14 days, perfect days.
- `Templates/` — `Monthly Habit Tracker` (blank grid) and `Habit Log Entry` (daily log).

## First steps

1. Open [[2026-05 Habits]] and read the grid like a chart: you can spot the weekend pattern and the mid-month dip without a single query.
2. Install **Dataview** (see README), then open [[Habit Dashboard]] and watch the same data become streaks and totals. For the streaks block, enable *JavaScript queries* in Dataview's settings.
3. Create `2026-06 Habits` in `Habits/` and insert the `Monthly Habit Tracker` template. Rename the columns to **your** five habits — keep at most five.
4. Tonight, log day one with the `Habit Log Entry` template. The whole ritual takes under a minute.
5. Delete the April/May examples whenever you are done learning from them (delete both the monthly notes and `Daily Log` — the dashboard will then show only your data).

## Plugins

- **Dataview** — required only for [[Habit Dashboard]]. The monthly grids work with zero plugins.

> [!tip] Track the keep, not the gap
> Missed days get a quiet `·`, not a red mark, and a broken streak just starts a new one. The grid's job is honesty, not guilt — the difference is what keeps you logging in week three.
