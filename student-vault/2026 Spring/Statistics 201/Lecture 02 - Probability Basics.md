# Lecture 02 - Probability Basics

## Notes

**What probability is.** A number from 0 to 1 expressing how likely an event is: 0 impossible, 1 certain. For equally likely outcomes, probability = favorable outcomes ÷ total outcomes (one fair die showing a 4: one favorable of six total).

**Vocabulary**
- *Experiment* — a process with an uncertain outcome (rolling a die).
- *Sample space* — all possible outcomes.
- *Event* — a subset of the sample space we care about.

**Combining events**
- *Complement* — probability an event does **not** happen = 1 minus the probability it does. Often the easy route to "at least one".
- *Independent events* — one's outcome does not affect the other's; the probability of both is found by multiplying.
- *Mutually exclusive events* — cannot both occur; the probability of either is found by adding.

**Common trap from lecture.** Independent ≠ mutually exclusive. Mutually exclusive events are actually *dependent* — if one happens, the other's probability drops to zero.
