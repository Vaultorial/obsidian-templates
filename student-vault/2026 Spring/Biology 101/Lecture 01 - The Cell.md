# Lecture 01 - The Cell

## Notes

**Cell theory — the foundation.** Three claims: all living things are made of cells; the cell is the basic unit of life; all cells come from pre-existing cells.

**Two broad cell types**
- *Prokaryotic* — no membrane-bound nucleus; DNA sits free in the cytoplasm. Bacteria and archaea. Generally smaller and simpler.
- *Eukaryotic* — DNA enclosed in a nucleus; has membrane-bound organelles. Plants, animals, fungi, protists.

**Key organelles (eukaryotic) and their jobs**
- *Nucleus* — holds DNA; control center for the cell.
- *Mitochondria* — release usable energy from nutrients (cellular respiration).
- *Ribosomes* — build proteins.
- *Endoplasmic reticulum* — transport and processing; rough ER (with ribosomes) handles proteins, smooth ER handles lipids.
- *Cell membrane* — selective barrier controlling what enters and leaves.
- Plant cells additionally have a *cell wall* (support) and *chloroplasts* (photosynthesis).
