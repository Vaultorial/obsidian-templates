# Website Content Plan

Supporting note for [[Personal Website Launch]]. The point of this note is to make scope visible, so the project cannot quietly grow.

## At launch

- **About page** — three short paragraphs, no résumé prose.
- **Now page** — what I am working on this season; updated quarterly at the [[Career]] review.
- **Five essays** — two reworked from old drafts, three new. Working titles:
    1. Why I keep a paper notebook next to a digital one
    2. Slow travel as a planning style (will borrow from [[Japan Itinerary Draft]])
    3. What batch cooking taught me about systems
    4. Notes on changing careers without burning the old one down
    5. One year of weekly reviews — what stuck

## Explicitly not at launch

- Newsletter signup. Comments. Analytics dashboards. Custom typography rabbit hole.
- A posting schedule. The site launches as a body of work, not a treadmill.

## Done means

All five essays read aloud once, about/now pages live, site loads on a phone.
