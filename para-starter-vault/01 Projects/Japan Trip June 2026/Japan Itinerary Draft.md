# Japan Itinerary Draft

Working skeleton for [[Japan Trip June 2026]]. Firm at the edges, soft in the middle — on purpose.

## Week 1 — Tokyo (booked)

- Days 1–2: land, recover, neighborhood walks only. No museums on jet lag.
- Day 3: old Tokyo day — Yanaka and Ueno on foot.
- Day 4: day trip out to Kamakura if the weather holds, otherwise swap with Day 5.
- Days 5–6: unplanned. Leaving deliberate gaps; the best days last trip were the empty ones.

## Week 2 — Kyoto + rural stop (open)

- Kyoto base for 4–5 nights, temples in the early morning before the crowds.
- Open question: **Takayama or Kanazawa** for the rural stop. Takayama wins on mountain scenery, Kanazawa on food and gardens. Decide by end of April so trains can be booked.

## Principles for this trip

1. One anchor activity per day, maximum.
2. Every third day has nothing scheduled.
3. Book trains and beds; leave everything else loose.
