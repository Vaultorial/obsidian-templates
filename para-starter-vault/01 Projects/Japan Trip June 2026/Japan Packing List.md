# Japan Packing List

Supporting note for [[Japan Trip June 2026]]. Rule: one carry-on each, June is rainy season, laundry mid-trip instead of more clothes.

## Carry

- [ ] Passport + printed booking confirmations (paper backup, learned this the hard way)
- [ ] Rail passes / transit card
- [ ] Packable rain shell — June is tsuyu, assume rain
- [ ] One pair of shoes that can do 20k steps wet
- [ ] Power adapter + short extension lead
- [ ] Small day bag that folds flat

## Deliberately not packing

- Umbrella — buy a cheap clear one there, leave it behind
- "Just in case" outfits — laundry day in Kyoto covers it
- Paper guidebook — notes from [[Travel MOC]] exported to phone instead

> [!tip] Pack-twice method
> Lay everything out a week early, remove a third, pack the rest the night before. The week-early pass is where the bad decisions get caught.
