# Clipping - batch cooking takeaways

My own notes after reading a piece on batch cooking last night. Captured fast, filing decision below.

- Cook components, not finished meals: a tray of roasted vegetables, a pot of grains and one sauce recombine all week without feeling like leftovers.
- The bottleneck is cleanup, not cooking — start the dishwasher before the second batch goes in the oven.
- Two hours on Sunday covers roughly four weekday dinners for us.
- Freeze in flat bags, not tubs: faster to thaw, easier to stack.
