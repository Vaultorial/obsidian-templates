# Spanish 30-Day Challenge

**Archived 2026-02-28.** Kept as a worked example of what lands in `04 Archive`: finished things, with a short honest post-mortem so the effort is not lost.

## What the project was

Thirty consecutive days of Spanish practice, twenty minutes a day, January into February. Originally framed as preparation for future travel.

## What actually happened

Nineteen of the thirty days. The streak broke twice in week three and never fully recovered, though practice continued unevenly to the end of February.

## Post-mortem

- **What worked:** doing it immediately after breakfast on the days it happened at all — accidental habit stacking before I had read about [[Habit Stacking]].
- **What failed:** the 30-day frame itself. A streak makes day 20 carry the weight of days 1–19, so one miss feels like collapse.
- **Verdict:** the next language attempt will be a boring untracked default ("after breakfast, ten minutes"), owned by an area, not a challenge project.

## Why it lives in Archive

It is finished. PARA archives without deleting: the post-mortem keeps paying rent — it is linked from [[Habits & Routines MOC]] and [[Travel MOC]] — while the dead project stops cluttering the active folders.
