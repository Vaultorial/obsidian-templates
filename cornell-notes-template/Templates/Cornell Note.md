# {{title}}

> [!cue] Cues
> Fill this column *after* the session. Turn each chunk of notes into a question or keyword. These prompts are what you test yourself against later.

> [!notes] Notes
> Capture here, live. Short lines, abbreviations, your own shorthand. Don't format while listening — just get it down.

> [!summary] Summary
> Two or three sentences, in your own words, written without looking back at the notes column. If you can't, you have found the gap — and that is the point.

> [!note]- How to use this (delete after reading)
> Layout comes from the `cornell` CSS snippet (enabled in this vault). During capture, write only in **notes**. Same day, fill **cue**, then **summary**. To review: cover the notes column, answer the cues aloud. Use the *Class Notes* variant for lectures with a course and professor.
