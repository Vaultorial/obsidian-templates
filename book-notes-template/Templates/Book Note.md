# {{title}}

## Summary

Three to five sentences, your own words, written within a day of finishing — what was the book actually arguing, and what stuck?

## Key ideas

Four to six bullets. Restate each idea as you would explain it, not as the book phrased it.

## Favorite quote

At most one, short, attributed. If nothing earns the spot, say so and skip it — a paraphrase that names the idea is worth more than a passage you collected.

## What changed because of it

The honest section. One concrete thing you do differently, or an admission that nothing changed — which usually explains the rating.
