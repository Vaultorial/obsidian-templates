# Show Your Work

## Summary

A short book against the myth of the lone genius who appears only with finished masterpieces. Kleon's alternative is working in public at small scale: share process, not just products — the sketches, the influences, the dead ends — and let an audience accumulate around the work rather than be chased after it. The advice is aimed at people who find self-promotion distasteful, which is exactly why it lands: showing the work is framed as generosity and documentation, not marketing.

## Key ideas

- Process is shareable long before products exist; "here is what I'm figuring out" is a legitimate contribution.
- Teach what you know — explaining your methods costs you nothing and compounds your reach.
- Credit your influences specifically and generously; lineage is part of the work.
- An imperfect daily share beats a perfect someday launch.

## Favorite quote

> "Share something small every day."
> — Austin Kleon

## What changed because of it

One small public note per week about work in progress — modest, but it broke the finished-things-only habit. A 3: genuinely useful, read in an afternoon, one idea doing most of the lifting.
