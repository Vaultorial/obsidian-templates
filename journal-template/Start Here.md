# Start Here

Welcome. This vault is a journal built around **bullet-journal rapid logging**: short symbol-prefixed lines instead of essays, a tiny gratitude section per day, and a monthly reflection to stitch the days together. Two real weeks of entries (May 25 – June 7, 2026) and a finished May reflection are included so you can feel the rhythm before writing a word.

## The rapid logging key

One line per item, symbol first:

•  task to do
×  task done
○  event (it happened, no action needed)
–  note, observation, stray thought
\> task migrated to another day

> [!important] The one Obsidian quirk
> Type the migration symbol as `\>` (backslash + angle bracket). A bare `>` at the start of a line turns into a quote block in Obsidian; the backslash keeps it a plain `>` in reading view. Point migrations at a day: `\> Fix the lid → [[2026-06-06]]`, then re-log the task as `•` on that day. The examples show this twice — follow a casserole dish through [[2026-05-25]] → [[2026-05-26]].

## How the vault is organized

- `Journal/2026/05/` and `Journal/2026/06/` — entries named `YYYY-MM-DD`, one folder per month, plus the monthly reflection (`2026-05 Reflection`).
- `Templates/` — entry templates in three flavors (bullet journal, daily journal prose, Templater with prev/next links) and a `Monthly Reflection` template.

The core **Daily notes** plugin is configured to create today's entry in the right month folder automatically (format `YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD`), pre-filled from the bullet journal template.

## First steps

1. Read the two example weeks in order, starting at [[2026-05-25]]. Notice how short the lines are — that brevity is what keeps a journal alive on tired days.
2. Read [[2026-05 Reflection]] to see what the daily entries are *for*.
3. Click the calendar ribbon icon: today's entry appears, key included. Log three lines and one gratitude. Done.
4. On the last day of the month, create `2026-06 Reflection` in `Journal/2026/06/` from the `Monthly Reflection` template.

## Plugins

None required. Optional:

- **Templater** — powers the `Journal Entry (Templater)` variant with automatic yesterday/tomorrow links. Install it, set `Templates` as its template folder, and switch the Daily notes template setting to that variant.

> [!tip] Lower the bar
> A two-line entry is a kept habit; a blank day is fine too — just don't backfill fiction. The examples include mundane lines on purpose: the magic is in rereading a month of them.
