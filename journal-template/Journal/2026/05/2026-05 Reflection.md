# May 2026 — Monthly Reflection

## What this month held

The allotment went from plan to plants: tomatoes out by [[2026-05-25|the holiday Monday]], peas tied, first flowers showing by [[2026-05-30|month's end]]. At work, the summer reading program stopped being a pile of sticky notes and became a poster, a schedule and a start date. Quieter than April, in the best way.

## What felt heavy

The catalogue backlog hung over the first half of the month like weather. It only moved once I gave it the first twenty minutes of every shift instead of "whenever there's time" — there is never time, there are only first twenty minutes.

## What I want to change in June

One screen-free evening per week became three almost by accident once the garden needed me. Making it official: Tuesdays and Thursdays, phone stays in the hallway drawer.

## Small moments worth keeping

- The robin supervising the planting from the fence post.
- A second-grader bowing to the library.
- Strawberries at the market stall, eaten before we reached the next stall.

## One sentence for the month

May was the month things took root — most of them on purpose.
