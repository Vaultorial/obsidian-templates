# Odette Varga

## Appearance

Fifties, flour on her sleeves, keeps a ledger tucked in her apron.

## Personality

- **Trait:** Remembers every face and every unpaid tab.
- **Ideal:** A roof and a hot meal for anyone who pays or works.
- **Bond:** The inn was her late wife's; she will not lose it.
- **Flaw:** Cannot resist a piece of gossip, even a dangerous one.

## Voice and mannerisms

Calls everyone "love". Taps the ledger when she wants payment.

## What they want

To keep the Ferrymen's Guild from raising the bridge toll again.

## What they know

Which rooms the guild's men rented last month, and under what names.

## Adventure hooks

- Hires the party to find out who has been watering down her ale deliveries.
- Asks them to carry a sealed letter to Brother Aldous at the Old Chapel.

## Relationships

- [[Fennick Shaw]]: owes him money
- [[Wren Talbot]]: lets her brother sleep in the stable
