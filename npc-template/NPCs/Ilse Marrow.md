# Ilse Marrow

## Appearance

Tall, grey braid, lantern-shaped pin on a patched coat.

## Personality

- **Trait:** Answers questions with questions.
- **Ideal:** The law protects the town, even when the town does not deserve it.
- **Bond:** Her younger brother rows for the Ferrymen's Guild.
- **Flaw:** Looks away when the guild is involved, to keep him safe.

## Voice and mannerisms

Short sentences. Never sits down in someone else's house.

## What they want

Proof against the guild that does not lead back to her brother.

## What they know

That two bodies pulled from the river this year were not drownings.

## Adventure hooks

- Offers the party a quiet reward for evidence about the river bodies.
- Arrests one of them on a guild complaint, then lets slip why.

## Relationships

- [[Fennick Shaw]]: suspects him, cannot prove it
- [[Mira Quell]]: searching for her
