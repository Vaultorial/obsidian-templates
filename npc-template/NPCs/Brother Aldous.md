# Brother Aldous

## Appearance

Barefoot, ash on his brow, hands stained with candle wax.

## Personality

- **Trait:** Speaks to the dead as if they were in the room.
- **Ideal:** Every soul deserves a name on the stone.
- **Bond:** The unmarked graves behind the chapel.
- **Flaw:** Trusts no one who arrives by boat.

## Voice and mannerisms

Long pauses. Finishes other people's sentences wrongly.

## What they want

Names for the two men buried without them.

## What they know

The river bodies had guild tattoos burned off.

## Adventure hooks

- Asks the party to read the chapel's old burial register, which is missing pages.
- Refuses to talk while Captain Marrow is present.

## Relationships

- [[Fennick Shaw]]: rowed with him, years ago
