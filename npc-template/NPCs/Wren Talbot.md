# Wren Talbot

## Appearance

Twelve or so, oversized boots, a gull feather in her cap.

## Personality

- **Trait:** Never gives her real age.
- **Ideal:** A deal is a deal, even with grown-ups.
- **Bond:** Her little brother, who sleeps in the inn's stable.
- **Flaw:** Steals small things she does not need.

## Voice and mannerisms

Talks fast, counts coins out loud.

## What they want

Enough silver to get her brother apprenticed.

## What they know

Who goes in and out of the guild warehouse at night.

## Adventure hooks

- Sells the party a rumour for a copper, and a better one for silver.
- Goes missing after telling them too much.

## Relationships

- [[Odette Varga]]: runs errands for her
