# Fennick Shaw

## Appearance

Rings on every finger, soft hands, a boatman's tan he no longer earns.

## Personality

- **Trait:** Generous in public, exact in private.
- **Ideal:** Whoever controls the crossing controls the town.
- **Bond:** His guild, which he built from three leaky boats.
- **Flaw:** Underestimates anyone who looks poor.

## Voice and mannerisms

Laughs before he answers. Uses your name too often.

## What they want

To buy the bridge charter from the council before winter.

## What they know

Every cargo that has crossed the river in ten years.

## Adventure hooks

- Offers the party work escorting a cargo he will not open.
- Invites them to dinner to learn what Captain Marrow knows.

- [[Mira Quell]]: client, now a loose end
