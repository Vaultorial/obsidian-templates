- **Look:** Thick shawl, counts with an abacus on a string.
- **Wants:** Exact change, always.
- **Voice:** Repeats the price twice, slower the second time.
- **Secret:** Keeps a second ledger for Shaw.

Promote to a full NPC when the party comes back to them.
