# Weekly Reset Checklist

Sunday evening, twenty minutes, music on. The reset is what keeps [[Home]] trustworthy — a dashboard is only as good as the data underneath it.

## The loop

1. Open [[Home]] and look at **Today** and **Coming up**. Anything stale gets a new date or gets deleted — no zombie tasks.
2. Walk through the three active projects ([[Balcony Garden Summer Setup]], [[Portfolio Website Refresh]], [[Conversational Spanish Course]]). Add next week's tasks with `[due:: ]` dates while the context is loaded.
3. Skim [[Health & Fitness]] and [[Home Maintenance]] for anything the week shook loose.
4. Five minutes of inbox-to-vault: stray phone notes and screenshots become real notes or get deleted.
5. Close with the calendar: anything in next week that needs a task gets one now.

## Sunday extras (optional, seasonal)

- Pair the reset with the long ride — market loop out, podcast loop home.
- Water the balcony if the sky hasn't done it; check the [[Tomato Sauce That Works]] ingredient list against the pantry before the market.

## The one rule

If the reset gets skipped, the week still happens — just dumber. Skip it twice in a row and the first reset back doubles as an amnesty: bulk-reschedule without guilt.
