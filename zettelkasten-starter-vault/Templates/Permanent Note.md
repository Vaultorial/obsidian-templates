# State the idea as a claim

One idea per note. Two paragraphs maximum, in your own words: first the claim itself, then why it holds or what follows from it. If a second idea creeps in, it wants its own note.

**Related**

Link at least one existing permanent note, with a phrase saying *how* it relates — supports, contradicts, generalizes, is an instance of. The phrase is where the thinking happens.
