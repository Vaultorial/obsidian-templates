# Elaboration links new ideas to old ones

Elaboration means expressing a new idea in terms of what you already know — restating it, generating your own examples, asking how it relates to and differs from its neighbors. Memory seems to hold ideas by their connections; an unconnected fact has no handles to pull it back by, while an elaborated one can be reached along many routes.

This is the bridge between the memory thread and the understanding thread of this vault: retrieval and spacing strengthen a trace that already exists, elaboration determines how rich the trace is in the first place. It is also why a Zettelkasten works at all — forcing every new note to link to old notes *is* elaboration, performed in writing.
