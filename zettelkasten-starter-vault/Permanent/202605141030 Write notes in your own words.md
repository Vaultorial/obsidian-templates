# Write notes in your own words

Copying a passage stores the author's sentence; rewriting it stores the idea. Restating something forces a comprehension test that highlighting lets you skip — you cannot paraphrase what you have not understood, which is exactly why paraphrasing feels harder and why the difficulty is the point. A note I cannot write in my own words is a note about something I do not yet know.

The rule doubles as quality control for this whole vault: if a candidate permanent note is just a quotation with a link, it goes back to staging until it can stand in my words.
