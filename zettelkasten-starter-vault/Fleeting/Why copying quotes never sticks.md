# Why copying quotes never sticks

Caught myself highlighting a whole page again. I can copy a passage and feel like I own it, but a week later I can't even paraphrase it. The copying *feels* like work but apparently isn't the part that counts.

Connects to what Ahrens says about writing as thinking — see [[How to Take Smart Notes - Ahrens]].
