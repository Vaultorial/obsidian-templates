# Moonwalking with Einstein - Foer

Literature note, my words throughout. A journalist spends a year training with competitive memorizers and reports from the inside.

## The argument as I understood it

- The mnemonic techniques used in memory competitions are old — the method of loci goes back to classical antiquity, traditionally credited to the Greek poet Simonides.
- The core trick is translation: human memory is comparatively weak for abstract symbols but strong for places and vivid images, so memorizers convert whatever they must retain into imagery planted along a familiar spatial route.
- Foer's own progress suggests these are trainable skills rather than gifts, though he is candid that the techniques are narrow — superb for ordered lists, mostly irrelevant for understanding.
