# Make It Stick - Brown Roediger McDaniel

Literature note, my words throughout. A book on how learning actually works, written against the most popular study habits.

## The argument as I understood it

- The strategies that feel best — rereading, highlighting, massed repetition — produce fluency with the *text*, which learners mistake for mastery of the *material*.
- Practices that feel worse in the moment tend to durably work better: spacing study sessions out, testing yourself instead of reviewing, and mixing problem types rather than blocking them.
- The discomfort is not a side effect; effortful retrieval and switching are presented as the very thing that strengthens memory.
