# Website Redesign Kickoff

## Agenda

1. Why now: what the current site costs us
2. Scope and non-goals
3. Roles, cadence, tooling
4. Discovery plan for April

## Notes

- Sarah walked through the case: the site still leads with the retired product line, the content team cannot edit service pages without Tom, and mobile performance complaints come up in sales calls.
- Agreed scope: messaging, templates, accessibility, redirects. Explicit non-goals: no brand refresh, no blog re-platforming, no new CMS — we rebuild templates on the current one.
- Cadence: weekly 30-minute sync on Thursdays, design reviews at each design milestone, decisions recorded in these notes rather than in chat.
- Discovery: Priya runs stakeholder interviews (sales, support, the two newest hires as fresh eyes); Tom pulls the analytics and crawl data for the content audit.

## Decisions

- Rebuild on the existing CMS; revisit only if template work uncovers a blocker.
- Sarah is the single approver for copy; Helen for anything affecting recruitment pages.
- Launch target set for 2026-06-26, ahead of the summer campaign.
